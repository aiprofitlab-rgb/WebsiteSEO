### 2. Canonical Syndication (Double Indexing without Penalty)
When publishing long-form technical content on **Hashnode** and **Dev.to**, always specify the original `aiprofitlab.io` blog post URL in the canonical tag/frontmatter (`canonical_url`). This allows you to leverage the immense domain authority of these platforms for distribution while guaranteeing that Google credits `aiprofitlab.io` as the authoritative source.

### 3. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
AI search engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Gemini, Claude) heavily crawl community hubs and authoritative Q&A platforms like **Reddit**, **Quora**, and **Medium**. Answering targeted SME automation queries on these platforms directly builds brand citation equity in AI-generated answers.
