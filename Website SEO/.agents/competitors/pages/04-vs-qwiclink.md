## H1: AI Profit Lab vs QwicLink

### TL;DR

QwicLink is a tool you run. We're a service that runs it for you. QwicLink is
significantly cheaper — around OMR 617 in your first year on their AI tier against
about OMR 1,400 on ours — and if your needs fit inside their product and someone on
your team will own it, they're the better buy and we'd say so. We're the better fit
when nobody has time to run software, or when the work you need automated goes beyond
enquiries and booking into invoicing, data sync between your existing systems, and
dashboards.

---

### H2: The actual difference

Nearly every feature comparison between us would be misleading, because we're not the
same kind of product.

**QwicLink sells software.** You subscribe, you log in, you configure your pipelines,
set up your booking calendar, write your FAQ responses, connect your channels, and
maintain it as your business changes. It's built well for that and priced accordingly.

**We sell an outcome.** We run a discovery audit, work out where your business is
actually losing money, build the system, then monitor and improve it every month. You
don't log in to configure anything. Your team gets trained. When something breaks, it's
our problem.

That distinction determines almost everything else — including why we cost more.

### H2: Where QwicLink is the better choice

We'd rather you hear this from us than feel misled later.

- **Your budget is genuinely tight.** OMR 15–49/month against our OMR 75 plus setup is a
  real difference for a small business, and their product is competent.
- **You or someone on your team likes running tools.** If configuring a pipeline sounds
  fine rather than daunting, you don't need us — you'd be paying for management you
  won't value.
- **Your need is exactly booking and enquiries.** Clinics, salons, gyms, coaches with
  straightforward appointment flows. That's their target market and they built for it.
- **You want to start this week.** Self-serve means immediate. Our Starter takes 3–6 weeks
  because there's a build and an audit involved.
- **You want to test the idea cheaply before committing.** Sensible. Spend OMR 15 for a
  month, see whether automation helps your business at all, then decide.

---

### H2: Where we're the better choice

- **Nobody has time to run software.** The most common reason clients come to us. A tool
  nobody configures returns nothing, which makes cheap the most expensive option.
- **Your bottleneck isn't the inbox.** Invoices typed by hand, data re-entered between
  systems, no visibility on your numbers. A CRM tool doesn't reach any of that.
- **You need your existing systems connected.** We do custom API work between the tools
  you already run. Self-serve platforms connect to what they support, and no further.
- **You want the problem diagnosed first.** We audit before building, because the
  bottleneck an owner describes and the one costing the most money are often different.
- **You want your team trained.** Included with us, at OMR 100 a session standalone.
  Adoption is where most automation quietly dies.
- **You want one accountable person.** Founder-led, with a phone number. Not a ticket queue.

### H2: Moving from QwicLink to us

If you started with QwicLink and outgrew it, migration is usually straightforward —
both stacks sit on the same family of underlying platform, so contacts, pipelines and
calendars typically export cleanly by CSV.

What transfers: contacts, pipeline stages, booking history, message history where the
platform exports it. What needs rebuilding: automations and AI responses, because we
rebuild those against your audit findings rather than porting them. What we handle:
the whole migration, inside the setup fee.

### H2: FAQ

**Is QwicLink cheaper than AI Profit Lab?**
Yes. Their AI tier is roughly OMR 617 in year one against about OMR 1,400 for our
Starter. The difference is that they sell you a tool to operate and we operate a system
for you.

**Can I use both?**
In principle, though it's rarely worth it — you'd be paying two vendors for overlapping
functions. Pick the model that matches how involved you want to be.

**What if I try QwicLink first and it doesn't work out?**
That's a perfectly reasonable path, and it makes our discovery conversation better —
you'll know exactly which parts you couldn't get working and why.

**Do you both handle Arabic?**
Both of us run bilingual Arabic and English. Neither of us trains dialect-native models
the way [Nuqta](https://nuqtai.com/en) does. Test with your customers' real messages
before committing to any provider.
