### H2: What to actually compare on

Most people start by comparing features. Features are the least useful axis, because
almost everyone lists the same ones — WhatsApp, chatbot, CRM, AI. Five questions
separate these companies far more sharply:

**1. Who operates the system after it's built?**
This is the biggest fork in the market. Some options hand you a tool and a login.
Others run it for you. Both are legitimate. But if nobody in your business wants to
own a tool, a cheap subscription you never configure is the most expensive option
you can buy.

**2. Does the scope stop at the conversation?**
A lot of "AI automation" in Oman means replying to messages. That's valuable — but if
your bottleneck is invoicing, data entry between apps, or not knowing your numbers,
a chat tool won't touch it. Be clear about which problem you're solving.

**3. Where does your data live?**
For most SMEs, cloud is fine. For a bank, a ministry, a clinic or anyone under
sector-specific rules, it isn't. That single requirement eliminates most of this list
and points straight at the on-premise specialists.

**4. Is the Arabic real, or translated?**
There's a genuine difference between a model that handles Gulf Arabic natively and
one that translates into it. Ask for a live test in your customers' actual dialect
before you sign anything. Don't accept a demo in Modern Standard Arabic.

**5. What's the total first-year cost, not the monthly price?**
Setup fees change the picture completely. A OMR 49/month tool with a OMR 29 setup is
~OMR 617 in year one. A OMR 75/month service with a OMR 500 setup is ~OMR 1,400.
That gap is real and you should weigh it against what's included.

**Pricing** (published, in OMR): Social Presence — OMR 15/month + OMR 19 setup.

Custom Growth System is scope-priced. No long-term contract.

**Strengths:** Comfortably the best price in the market, and they publish it openly —
which is more than most of this list does. Genuine multi-channel inbox out of the box.
Bilingual. Built specifically for Oman. Mobile app. Low commitment, so trying it costs

**Weaknesses:** It's a tool, so configuration, maintenance and fixes land on you or

included. Their AI positioning is conservative — approved FAQ handling and
qualification rather than a deeply trained assistant. And their site uses illustrative
personas rather than named client case studies, so there's little external proof to check.

**Best for:** A small service business that wants cheap, fast lead capture and booking
and has someone in-house willing to own the tool.

**Not for:** Owners with no time or appetite to run software, or anyone whose real

### H2: Questions worth asking any provider on this list

1. Who operates and maintains the system after launch — and what happens when it breaks

2. Can I see it handling my customers' actual Arabic dialect, live, not in a recorded demo?
3. What's my total first-year cost including setup, and what's the exit process?
4. Which of my existing tools will this connect to, and what happens to the data if I leave?
5. Can I speak to a business like mine that you've done this for?
