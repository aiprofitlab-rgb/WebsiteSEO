Refund & Cancellation Policy

AI Profit Lab, a brand of Lotus Gulf International (لوتس الخليج العالمية ش ش و), CR 1570092, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. Last updated: 18 August 2026.

What we sell is custom digital work — websites, online storefronts, chatbots and automation built for one specific business. Work like that can't be boxed up and posted back, so this page sets out plainly when you get your money back, how much, and how long it takes.

Short version: if we haven't started, you get everything back. If we've started, you pay only for what's actually been done. If we deliver something that doesn't match what we agreed in writing and we can't fix it, you get everything back.

Before we start work

Full refund. No deduction, no explanation needed, no hard feelings.

"Starting work" has a specific meaning here: it's the day we begin building after your brief is confirmed, and we tell you in writing on that day. Until you have that message, nothing has started and your money is fully refundable.

After work has started

You pay for the work that has actually been completed up to the day you cancel, and the unused balance comes back to you.

We send you an itemised summary of what was done before we take anything — you see the arithmetic, not just a number. In practice, if you paid a 50% deposit and we're less than halfway through, you get the difference back.

If what we deliver doesn't match what we agreed

We fix it. Free, and for as many rounds as it takes, as long as it's within the written brief you approved.

If we genuinely can't make it match that brief, you get a full refund and you owe nothing. We'd rather return the money than argue about a site you don't want.

What we can't refund

Money already spent on your behalf. A domain name, a paid template or plugin licence, ad spend — once these are bought, we can't get them back either. We always show you the receipt, and we always ask before spending.

The included first year of hosting and care, once your site is live. It's part of the one-time price, not a subscription, so it isn't refunded month by month. The site remains yours either way.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@aiprofitlab.io or send a WhatsApp message to +968 9924 5250, with your invoice or reference number. That's it — there's no form to fill in and no phone queue. You'll be dealing with the person who did the work.

We acknowledge every request within one business day.

How you get the money back

  • Refunds are processed within three banking days of the amount being agreed.
  • The money always goes back to the same card or bank account used for the original payment. We can't send it to a different account, and we never refund in cash.
  • If you paid by card, your bank may take a few extra days to show the refund on your statement. That part is out of our hands.
  • Refunds are made in Omani Rial (OMR), the currency you were charged in.

If you're unhappy with the outcome

Talk to us first — most of this gets sorted out in one conversation. If you paid by card and you're still not satisfied, you have the right to raise the matter with your card issuer, but we'd ask you to give us the chance to fix it before you do.

This policy is governed by the laws of the Sultanate of Oman, and it sits alongside the service agreement you signed. Where the two differ, the signed agreement wins.
