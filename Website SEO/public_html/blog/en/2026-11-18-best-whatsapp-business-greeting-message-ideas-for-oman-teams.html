Questions people ask

What is an automated WhatsApp Business greeting message?

An automated WhatsApp Business greeting message is a pre-configured message sent automatically to prospective customers when they initiate contact with your business account. It delivers an instant acknowledgment, confirms operational availability, and provides introductory service details before a human representative joins the chat.

When does WhatsApp Business trigger a greeting message?

WhatsApp triggers a greeting message when a customer messages your business phone number for the first time, or after 14 days of consecutive chat inactivity. If an active conversation occurred recently, the platform suppresses repetitive greetings to avoid conversational friction.

Should commercial teams in Oman send greetings in Arabic, English, or both?

Bilingual greetings combining Arabic and English are strongly recommended for businesses in Muscat and across the GCC. Providing Arabic greetings respects Omani cultural norms, while including English ensures multinational corporate buyers and expatriate residents can navigate your services without communication barriers.

How do I configure a greeting message in the WhatsApp Business mobile application?

To configure a greeting message, open WhatsApp Business settings, navigate to Business Tools, and select Greeting Message. Enable the toggle, compose your customized text, specify recipient rules such as everyone or non-contacts, and save your changes. Your mobile device must maintain active internet access to deliver automated replies.

What is the difference between a greeting message and an away message on WhatsApp?

A greeting message triggers on initial customer contact or after prolonged inactivity regardless of time. In contrast, an away message activates specifically outside scheduled business hours or when team members are designated as unavailable, setting clear expectations for when staff will respond.

Can WhatsApp Business greeting messages include interactive buttons and quick replies?

Yes, but interactive buttons and list menus require connecting the WhatsApp Business Cloud API rather than using the basic mobile app. The Cloud API enables structured interactive elements that allow customers to select departments, choose languages, or request quotes with a single tap.

How does Oman Personal Data Protection Law apply to WhatsApp greeting messages?

Oman Personal Data Protection Law promulgated under Royal Decree 6/2022 requires commercial enterprises to obtain customer consent before collecting, storing, or processing personal data in CRM databases. Greeting messages should maintain transparency, avoid deceptive marketing, and refrain from enrolling contacts into promotional campaigns without explicit opt-in.

What essential information should a real estate or automotive greeting collect in Oman?

Greeting templates for high-value sales should prompt buyers for their full name, preferred transaction type such as buying or leasing, target geographic location within Oman, and estimated budget. Gathering these four data points immediately enables sales representatives to prepare tailored proposals before engaging.

How much should an SME in Muscat budget for WhatsApp API greeting automation?

For small and mid-sized enterprises in Oman, management should budget between 200 and 500 OMR for initial API setup and 20 to 50 OMR monthly for recurring cloud software subscriptions. Actual expenditure depends on incoming inquiry volume, CRM integration depth, and automated workflow complexity.

Automating business communication modernizes commercial operations, improves customer responsiveness, and enhances private-sector productivity across the Sultanate. These operational efficiencies directly advance the digital economy, economic diversification, and technological modernization priorities championed under Oman Vision 2040.
