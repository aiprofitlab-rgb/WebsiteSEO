✶ The three steps

From first conversation to a system that runs itself.

1 Find the leak

I look

We sit together — your office or a call, 30 to 60 minutes. I listen and ask simple questions:

  • Which tasks do you repeat every day?
  • Where do your hours actually go?
  • What would you rather be doing?
  • What’s stopping you from growing?

COSTS NOTHING · NO OBLIGATION

2 Seal it

I build

Within days — not months — you get a working version of your system:

  • You receive a link and test it yourself
  • You tell me what to change
  • I adjust until it’s 100% right
  • It connects to the tools you already use — WhatsApp, Excel, your invoicing

FIRST VERSION IN 1–3 WEEKS

3 Keep it flowing

I stay

The system goes live in your real business, and I don’t disappear:

  • I train your team until they’re confident
  • I watch the system run and fix what breaks
  • You get monthly reports in plain numbers
  • You have my WhatsApp

MONTH AFTER MONTH · NOT A ONE-TIME PROJECT

Why this won’t end up abandoned like the Excel sheet you built last year: nobody on your team has to type anything in. The systems read what your business already produces — the messages, orders and invoices that happen anyway. Tools die when they need feeding. These don’t need feeding.
