✶ The story

I didn’t read about these problems. I had them.

I’m an engineer. Before AI Profit Lab, I built a medical supplies distribution company from zero — it’s still running today.

Inventory that didn’t match the spreadsheet. Suppliers on credit terms I had to track in my head. Clients who quietly stopped ordering — and I’d find out a month later. A bank asking for numbers I couldn’t produce without three days of digging. That was my normal, the same as it’s probably yours.

So I started building systems for my own company. A dashboard, because I was tired of learning about problems late. Automatic follow-up, because inquiries were dying in an inbox overnight. Each one existed because a real business — mine — was leaking money without it.

The big vendors sell software built for corporations with IT departments. I build for the owner who answers their own WhatsApp.

I moved to Muscat and founded AI Profit Lab to build those same systems for businesses here — built for GCC businesses — not Silicon Valley ones — and working in both English and Arabic. The mission hasn’t changed: simple systems that actually work, for the people who need them most.

— Nahid Aby, Founder
