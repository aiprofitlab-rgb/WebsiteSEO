✼ Reasonable questions

Things worth asking a stranger with a flyer.

Is the "seats left" number real, or is it theatre?

Real. It's read live from a ledger every time this page loads, and a seat only counts when a 50% deposit has actually cleared into the company account. Submitting the form doesn't move it. If the ledger is unreachable, the page shows you nothing rather than a made-up number — you may have already seen that happen.

Why does the price go up? That feels like pressure.

Because one person builds these, one at a time. Early clients get a lower price for taking a risk on someone without a published local track record; once that proof exists, the discount has done its job. If the ladder ever stops reflecting real capacity, it stops being honest, and the whole point of this business is being the one you can believe.

What happens after I submit the form?

You get an email straight away with your reference and the deposit amount. Then, within one business day, I send the agreement and the invoice with payment details. You pay the 50% deposit, upload the transfer receipt on your claim page, and I confirm your seat once the money lands. The balance is due when the site goes live.

How do the pledge discounts actually work?

You tick what you're willing to do after the site is live. Nothing comes off the invoice up front — you pay the full price, and each pledge you deliver is paid back to you. If you don't like the site, or change your mind, you keep your money and I don't chase you. The pledge is written into your agreement so both sides know exactly what was promised.

Are there monthly fees?

No. It's a one-time price, and it includes a full year of hosting, security and care. After that year you can renew the care plan or not — the site is yours either way.

How long does it take?

About a week from the point I have your content and answers. The delay is almost never the building; it's waiting for photos and product lists.

Is the site really in Arabic and English?

Yes — the whole interface, and the AI agent answers in whichever language the buyer writes in. The Arabic is written properly, not run through a translator and left there.
