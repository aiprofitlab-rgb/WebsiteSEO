SEO — how Google decides who to show

Picture a library with ten million books and one librarian. Somebody walks in and asks for “the best water pump supplier in Muscat.” She does not read ten million books. She reaches for the few she already knows, already trusts, and has watched people come back to happy. SEO is the work of becoming a book she reaches for.

GEO — the same thing, for AI

When your buyer asks ChatGPT “who should I buy from in Oman?”, ChatGPT does not go and read the internet on the spot. It answers out of what it has already read and already trusts. GEO is the work of being inside what it read — so your name is in the answer itself, not on page four of something nobody opens.

And here is the part nobody tells you: it never finishes

Your competitors do not stop. Google changes its mind about who to trust almost daily. And the AI models are retrained and re-read the web on their own schedule — every time they do, the answer is written again from nothing.

So visibility is not a wall you build once. It is a garden. Stop watering it and it does not stay the way you left it. It goes back to weeds, because the person next door kept watering theirs.
