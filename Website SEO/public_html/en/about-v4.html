✶ The short version

I am an engineer who has built and run a real distribution business — importing, stocking, quoting, chasing. Not a consultant who read about it.

The problem I kept hitting was never strategy. It was a message that arrived at 9pm and got answered at 8 the next morning, by which time the buyer had already ordered somewhere else. Multiply that by a year and it is not a small number.

So I started building the thing I wanted: something that answers in both languages, knows the stock and the terms, and hands me only the buyers worth my time. AI Profit Lab is that, built for other owners in the same position.

Nahid Abyari · Founder

✶ Being useful about fit

Who I turn away.

Saying this on a public page costs me enquiries. It also saves us both a wasted month.

Enterprise and ERP-scale buyers

Different weight class, long procurement. You want a systems integrator, and you should have one.

Anyone shopping purely on price

I am not the cheapest option in Oman and will not pretend to be. I compete on the work being done for you.

Businesses with nothing digital yet

If there is no WhatsApp presence and no buyer flow at all, there is nothing here to automate yet.

“Make us go viral”

Wrong problem and wrong measure. I build systems that answer buyers, not campaigns that chase attention.
