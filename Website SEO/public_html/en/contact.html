✶ The awkward questions

Asked and answered.

How much does it cost?

Smart Website: OMR 950 one-time. Dashboard +650, autopilot +900, all three 2,200. Every number is on the price list.

Is there a monthly fee?

Not to keep anything working. The first year of hosting and care is in the build price. Two monthly services are optional and neither is required: the Growth Desk at OMR 75/month, and the Visibility Desk — the ongoing SEO and GEO work — at OMR 300/month.

Do you have clients I can speak to?

Not yet, and I won’t pretend otherwise. That is why the guarantee is named and both demos are open to click: the dashboard and the buyer agent.

What if it doesn’t work?

The First Inquiry Promise: no real buyer inquiry within 30 days of going live and I rebuild it free. If you still don’t get one, you get your money back. The Visibility Desk carries its own named guarantee: 6 months in, if Google and ChatGPT still do not name you, I refund that service’s fees and keep working free until they do.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes — both languages first-class, with Arabic checked by a native reader before it ships.

Who am I actually contracting with?

Lotus Gulf International, CR 1570092, Bousher, Muscat. AI Profit Lab is the brand. Not VAT registered, so invoices carry no VAT line.

How do I pay?

By card on the checkout page, or by bank transfer in OMR — on start, in three payments, or only once it has produced a real inquiry. Every structure is set out on the services page.
